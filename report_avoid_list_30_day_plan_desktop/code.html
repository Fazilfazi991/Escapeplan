A structured sprint to test your B2B Service idea without spending a dime on development.

Overall Progress 0%
1

Week 1: Find the Customer

Identify exactly who has the problem and where they hang out.

  • List 10 specific target companies.
  • Find contact info for the key decision-maker at each.
2

Week 2: Smallest Offer

Craft a service that solves one tiny piece of their problem.

  • Write a 1-page plain text pitch.
  • Define the exact outcome you promise.
3

Week 3: Ask for Money

Pitch the offer and try to get paid before doing the work.

  • Send 10 personalized outreach messages.
  • Conduct at least 3 discovery calls.
4

Week 4: Decide

Review the feedback. Did they pay? Pivot or proceed.

  • Analyze objections if no sales were made.
  • Fulfill the service if payment was received.
