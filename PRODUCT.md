# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Cold-traffic aspiring founders balancing limited time, testing capital, and uncertainty about which business model fits their circumstances.

## Product Purpose

EscapePlan turns an adaptive assessment into a deterministic, personalized comparison of viable business paths. Success means the user recognizes their situation, understands why one path fits, and wants to compare alternatives before risking meaningful capital.

## Positioning

EscapePlan is not a generic idea list or a probability-of-success claim. It filters business paths through the user's money, time, strengths, working style, and trade-offs, then explains the resulting fit.

## Operating Context

Landing → adaptive assessment → reveal → Business DNA → strongest match → comparison paywall → ₹199 demo checkout → paid report.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Deterministic scoring and Batch 7/8 logic remain authoritative.
- Personalization uses only stored assessment state.
- Exact rankings, scores, costs, and comparisons appear only when backed by connected deterministic state.
- ₹199 is a one-time unlock; real payment is not connected in this prototype.
- Paid-report navigation is Overview, Matches, Money, Quit Plan, and 30 Days only.

## Brand Commitments

EscapePlan retains its indigo, warm-white, direct, practical direction. The post-quiz signature is the Escape Route Map: branching paths narrow under real constraints, one route becomes clear, and alternatives illuminate after unlock.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the live assessment and stored answer state. It contains no testimonials, verified success rates, outcome guarantees, or validated comparative ranking dataset; none may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Reveal progressively; each screen earns the next click.
- Show useful information gaps, never invented bait.
- Translate report architecture into customer questions.
- Use motion to explain state changes, not decorate.
- Keep payment framing honest, concise, and proportional to the capital decision.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard operable, readable from 320px, and respectful of `prefers-reduced-motion`.
