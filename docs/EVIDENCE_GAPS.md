# Evidence Gaps

Batch 8 improves traceability but does not complete model-level India diligence.

## Highest-priority gaps

1. City-specific premises, fit-out, deposit, utility, and trade-licence costs for restaurant, cloud kitchen, retail, kiosk and services.
2. Current equipment and working-capital quotes for manufacturing, automotive, vending, logistics and food production.
3. Franchise-specific disclosure, fee, capex and working-capital terms. “Franchise” is too broad for a firm consumer range.
4. State/local rules for shops and establishments, municipal trade licences, fire, pollution, signage and professional credentials.
5. Current marketplace commissions, logistics, returns and ad-cost economics by product category.
6. Model-specific validation and first-revenue windows backed by operator cohorts rather than planning assumptions.
7. Credible India margin/unit-economics benchmarks by model and niche.
8. Human expert calibration labels with multiple reviewers and disagreement tracking.

## Low-confidence model groups

Eighteen models remain low at model-summary level, concentrated in professional practice, physical retail, franchise, manufacturing, distribution, rental, local logistics, automotive, property/event/experience services, and other highly local formats. Their cross-cutting regulatory evidence is useful, but their capital/time ranges remain generalized.

## Prohibited inference

Do not treat a licence fee as total setup cost, a platform subscription as acquisition cost, an MSME classification threshold as recommended investment, or a project profile as a current vendor quote. Do not infer success rates.

## Research queue

For each high-priority physical model, collect at least three comparable India operator/vendor quotes across two cities, one official approval checklist for each relevant state/city pilot, and one experienced-operator review. Reissue affected evidence records and overlay versions rather than editing history.
