Batch 8 changes interpretation, not the core promise. Rankings remain deterministic and profile match remains separate from success probability and evidence confidence.

Readiness values are 50.0–82.7 for the original set except the zero-capital persona at 57.7 and zero-hours persona at 64.3. Readiness does not collapse solely because a particular model cannot be operated.

## Major behavior changes

- Capital now reports validation and launch states separately.
- Zero capital no longer excludes models with a documented no-cash or concept-only test.
- Explicit offline/online mismatch has a real cost, while no preference is neutral rather than perfect.
- Industry experience is a bounded skill input.
- Extreme income capacity contributes only 11% and cannot overpower operating mismatch.
- Evidence confidence is emitted beside match, not folded into it.

All major changes are traceable to factor deltas. No narrative model participates.
